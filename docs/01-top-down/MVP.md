Dưới đây là toàn bộ phạm vi "Scope MVP" (gộp [MVP] + [MVP-rút gọn]), đã loại bỏ hết các mục Phase 2/Không dùng, sẵn sàng để chuyển thành backlog sprint.

1. Student Meal Management

   - Student Meal Eligibility Management
     - Define Meal Eligibility Criteria
     - Determine Student Meal Eligibility
   - Meal Registration Management
     - Register for Meals
     - Modify Meal Registration
     - Cancel Meal Registration
     - Record Dietary Note at Registration
   - Meal Attendance Management
     - Record Meal Attendance
     - Monitor Meal Attendance                          [rút gọn]
2. Meal Planning & Menu Management

   - Dish Management
     - Define Dish
     - Manage Dish Information
   - Menu Management
     - Create Menu
     - Assign Dishes to Menu
     - Approve Menu                                     [rút gọn: 1 cấp duyệt]
   - Meal Schedule Management
     - Define Meal Schedule
     - Assign Menu to Schedule
3. Meal Operation

   - Meal Demand & Quantity Management
     - Determine Meal Demand
     - Calculate Expected Meal Quantity
     - Send Meal Order to Catering Vendor
   - Meal Receiving from Vendor
     - Record Delivered Quantity from Vendor
     - Inspect Delivered Meal Quality
     - Confirm Received Quantity
   - Meal Distribution
     - Record Distributed Quantity
   - Meal Reconciliation                                   [rút gọn]
     - Reconcile Ordered vs Delivered Quantity
     - Resolve Quantity Discrepancies                    [rút gọn]
4. Meal Fee & Cost Management

   - Meal Fee Configuration
     - Define Meal Fee
     - Set Effective Period
   - Meal Fee Assessment
     - Determine Chargeable Meals
     - Calculate Meal Fees
   - Meal Payment Management                                [rút gọn]
     - Record Meal Payment
     - Track Payment Status                               [rút gọn: chỉ 2-3 trạng thái đơn giản]
   - Meal Cost Management                                    [rút gọn]
     - Record Meal Costs
     - Calculate Meal Cost
5. Reporting & Transparency                                  [rút gọn]

   - Operational Reporting                                   [rút gọn]
     - Generate Daily Meal Operation Report
     - Generate Reconciliation Report                      [rút gọn: đối soát vendor]
   - Cost & Fee Reporting                                     [rút gọn]
     - Generate Fee Report
     - Generate Payment Report
     - Generate Cost Report                                [rút gọn: công nợ với catering]
   - Transparency Information Management                     [rút gọn]
     - Prepare Transparency Information
     - Publish Transparency Information
6. User & Access Management                                  [rút gọn]

   - User Account Management
     - Register User Account
     - Update User Information
   - Role & Permission Management                             [rút gọn]
     - Define Role                                          (Admin, Kế toán, Phụ trách bán trú, Phụ huynh)
     - Assign Permission to Role                            [rút gọn: gán quyền cố định theo role, không cho tùy biến]
     - Assign Role to User
7. Nutrition & Health Management                              [rút gọn]

   - Allergy & Dietary Restriction Management
     - Record Student Allergy/Dietary Restriction
     - Flag Restricted Ingredients in Menu
     - Alert on Menu-Restriction Conflict                   [rút gọn: cảnh báo đơn giản, chưa cần chặn tự động]
8. Master Data & System Configuration

   - Academic Structure Management
     - Manage School Year/Semeste
     - Manage Class & Grade Information
     - Manage Student Profile
   - System Configuration Management
     - Configure Lunch Serving Day
     - Configure Holiday/Non-Meal Day Calendar
