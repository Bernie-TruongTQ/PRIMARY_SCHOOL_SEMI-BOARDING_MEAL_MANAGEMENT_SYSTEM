## RACI Legend

- **R (Responsible):** The role that executes the activity or performs the daily data entry.
- **A (Accountable):** The role that has ultimate decision authority, approves changes, or verifies accuracy.
- **C (Consulted):** The role providing input, reasons, or receiving preliminary drafts.
- **I (Informed):** The role updated on progress or results through notifications and view-only dashboards.
